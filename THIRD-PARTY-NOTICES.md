## Herdr

The `issue-board` mod is written for [Herdr](https://herdr.dev) and follows patterns from the mods Herdr ships, such as the spinner and the agent status, and reuses some of their icons (below). Herdr is licensed under the Apache License 2.0; the full text is in `licenses/Apache-2.0.txt`.

These icons in `herdr/mods/issue-board/` are copied from Herdr's shipped mods:

- from `mods/workspace-chrome`: `check.svg`, `clock.svg`, `minus.svg`, `plus.svg`, `spinner.svg`, `x.svg`
- from `mods/tabs`: `win-close.svg`, `win-max.svg`, `win-min.svg`, `win-restore.svg`

`external.svg` is Lucide's `external-link` icon, and `refresh.svg` is a simplified version of Lucide's `rotate-cw`.

Herdr's own third-party notices credit the icons in its shipped mods to Lucide and Feather, under the licences below.
